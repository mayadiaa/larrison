Our Services

International sourcing, supplier qualification, private label support, regulatory documentation preparation, export logistics coordination and long-term supply agreements.

Road Safety Products – Import & Distribution

We import and distribute a comprehensive range of road safety and traffic management products including traffic cones and delineators, road barriers and channellers, reflective road studs and pavement markers, temporary and permanent traffic signage, and safety bollards and protective systems.

Medicinal Plants & Herbal Raw Materials – Export

We export selected medicinal plants and herbal raw materials for herbal medicine manufacturing, nutraceutical production and cosmetic and wellness formulations. Our sourcing model focuses on traceable origin, controlled harvesting, proper drying and storage, and consistent quality standards.

Food Supplements & Nutraceutical Products – Export

We supply finished and semi-finished food supplement and nutraceutical products to international distributors and wholesalers including herbal supplements, functional formulations, vitamins and private label ranges.

Quality & Compliance

We provide certificates of analysis, product specifications, origin and traceability documentation, packaging and labelling support, and full export documentation.
